## Snake, jet, or repair: what each one is actually for

Knowing the three levels keeps you from overpaying and from underfixing.

A snake, or cable machine, spins a head down the line and punches through a soft clog. It is the right tool for a simple, one-time blockage, and it is what the $89 cleaning uses on a standard drain. A snake does not remove grease coating the pipe wall or a root mass, and it does not fix a damaged pipe.

Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the full inside wall of the pipe, cutting roots and blasting away grease and scale. It lasts far longer than a snake for buildup and roots because it cleans the wall instead of boring a hole. It costs more than the $89 cleaning because it is a bigger job, and we only recommend it when the camera shows a cause a snake will not hold against.

A repair, whether a spot dig or a liner, is for a pipe that is cracked, collapsed, offset, or bellied. No amount of cleaning fixes a broken pipe. When the camera shows that, we show you, and we quote the repair.

## When the cheap fix is the right call

Here is the flip side. If your clog is a simple, accessible blockage and the camera comes back clean, the $89 cleaning is the whole fix and you are done. We are not going to invent a reason to jet a line that does not need it, and we will tell you when a snake solved it.
